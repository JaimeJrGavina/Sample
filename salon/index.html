Our Studio

Where salon artistry meets classic barbering

Velvet & Blade was founded by two friends, a colorist and a master barber, who wanted one place where everyone could get a great look without the attitude.

Our team trains every year in the latest techniques, and we only use professional, salon-grade products that are kind to your hair and scalp.

  • Precision cuttingTailored to your face and lifestyle
