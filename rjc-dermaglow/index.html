How do I book a consultation?

Send us a message on our Facebook page, call or text 0917 826 0553, or fill out the inquiry form on this page. Tell us the treatment you're interested in and we'll reply to confirm a schedule.

How much does a treatment cost?

Prices depend on the treatment and what your skin needs, so we confirm them at consultation. Message us and we can give you more details.

I'm not sure which treatment is right for me. Can you help?

Of course. That's what the consultation is for. Tell us what you'd like to improve and we'll explain your options. Okay lang kung first time mo; we'll guide you.

Will I see results after one session?

It depends on the treatment and on your skin. Some treatments are done over a series of sessions. We'll explain what to expect during your consultation. Results vary by individual.

What are your clinic hours?

Where is the clinic?
