# Credits & licenses: Kusina ni Aling Dalisay (demo site)

This is a **sample website for a fictional business**. The business name, prices, reviews, stats, phone number
(0917 000 1234), Messenger page (`m.me/samplepage`), address and email (`hello@example.com`) are all placeholders.

## Website template

| Item | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| "Grilli" restaurant template (HTML/CSS/JS), by codewithsadee | https://github.com/codewithsadee/grilli (commit `87881dc`) | MIT License. Full text in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). |

The MIT License allows commercial use, modification and client work, as long as the copyright notice and license
text stay with the code. They are kept in `LICENSE` and in the header of `assets/css/style.css`.

What changed from the template: new content and sections (stats, menu categories, bilao trays, sample reviews,
inquiry form, Messenger and call buttons, mobile floating bar, demo banner), and a separate override stylesheet
(`assets/css/ph.css`). The preloader and the Google Fonts and unpkg CDN links were removed. The template's own
images, logo and decorative PNG/SVG shapes are **not** used, because their origin isn't documented in the repo.
They were replaced with CSS patterns and with the photos listed below.
