Our Story

Parang luto ni Nanay

Aling Dalisay started with three kaldero on a folding table outside her house, feeding jeepney drivers and students on their way to school. Ngayon, may sarili na kaming kainan, but the recipes, the generous servings and the warm "Kain na!" haven't changed.
