Why get a home valuation?

Knowing your home's value helps you plan.

  • Plan your next move. See what you could have to work with for your next home, whether you're upsizing, downsizing or relocating.
  • Price it right from the start. A realistic price based on local sales helps you avoid sitting on the market or leaving money on the table.
  • Understand your equity. Useful when you're thinking about refinancing, renovating or your long-term finances.
  • Decide which updates are worth it. Learn which improvements may matter to buyers in your area, and which ones probably won't.

Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the home value estimate really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to sell or sign anything.

Is this an appraisal?

No. An online estimate is not an appraisal. It's an agent's opinion of value based on recent nearby sales, market conditions and the information you share. Lenders typically require a formal appraisal by a licensed appraiser.

How is my estimate calculated?

We look at recently sold and active homes similar to yours in size, age, location and condition, then adjust for your home's features and updates. An in-person or video walkthrough can make the estimate more precise.

How is this different from online "instant" estimates?

Automated tools use public records and formulas. They can't see upgrades, condition or what makes your street special. A local agent reviews those details personally.

Will I be pressured to sell?

Not at all. Many homeowners request an estimate just to stay informed. You decide if and when you'd like to talk next.

How soon will I get my estimate?

Usually within one business day. If we need a detail or two, we'll reach out first.

What happens to my information?

It's used only to prepare your estimate and follow up with you about it. We never sell your information, and you can opt out at any time.
