Our Story

Serving Manila since 2013

RJC Dermaglow and Aesthetic Medical Clinic was founded on April 18, 2013. For over a decade, the clinic has brought together dermatological, medical, dental, laboratory, and wellness services in one place — so caring for your skin, health, and smile doesn't mean running all over the city.

You'll find us at the Ramagi Building on Pedro Gil Street in Paco, Manila, with long clinic hours seven days a week — including evenings until 11:00 PM from Monday to Saturday.
