Our Story

Good coffee, good company

Harbor & Bean started as a small coffee cart with one espresso machine and a big dream. Today we're a full café and kitchen, but our promise hasn't changed: real ingredients, fair prices and a warm welcome for everyone who walks in. We work with local farmers and bakers, roast our own beans and cook everything to order.
