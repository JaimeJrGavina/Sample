# Credits & licenses: Harbor & Bean Café (demo site)

This is a **sample website for a fictional business**. The business name, prices, reviews, stats, phone number
((555) 014-2290), address (123 Sample Street, Anytown) and email (`hello@example.com`) are all placeholders.

## Website template

| Item | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| "Grilli" restaurant template (HTML/CSS/JS), by codewithsadee | https://github.com/codewithsadee/grilli (commit `87881dc`) | MIT License. Full text in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). |

The MIT License allows commercial use, modification and client work, as long as the copyright notice and license
text stay with the code. They are kept in `LICENSE` and in the header of `assets/css/style.css` and `assets/js/script.js`.

What changed from the template: new café content (sample stats, coffee/brunch/pastry menu with sample prices,
weekend special, catering & private events, sample reviews, reservation form with demo alert, hours table, map
placeholder), tap-to-call buttons in the header (round icon button on phones), a mobile bottom bar (Call Now +
Reserve) that sits below the hero buttons, a demo banner, and an override stylesheet (`assets/css/site.css`).
Scroll reveal is progressive: content is visible by default and only animates after the visitor starts scrolling;
all motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The preloader and the Google Fonts / unpkg CDN links were
removed. The template's own images, logo and decorative PNG/SVG shapes are **not** used, because their origin isn't
documented in the repo; they were replaced with CSS patterns and the photos below.
